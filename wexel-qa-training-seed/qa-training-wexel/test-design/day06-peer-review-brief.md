# Day 6 — Peer review brief (self-administered, no EM send)

A junior QA on your client team, Mika, sent you a draft test design for ticket **CN-125** (`test-design/day06-junior-CN-125-design.md`) and plans to share it with the PO tomorrow. You did the same ticket on Day 2. Mika and the draft are fictional training material.

Review it the way you would as Mika's senior. Time box: about 45 minutes.

## Deliverable (`test-design/day06-peer-review.md`)

1. **Findings, ranked by risk** (most harmful first). For each: what is wrong, which acceptance criterion or line it breaks, what goes wrong in production if it ships, and the fix or the correct value.
2. **One thing Mika did well**, specifically.
3. **Corrected amounts** for the two cases that matter most.
4. **Your message to Mika** (150 words or less) that would go with the review: claim → evidence → impact → ask, kind and specific, ending with a question or a next step for Mika.

Write it for Mika, not for your EM. Rewriting the whole design for them is not the goal: say what to change and why, so they can fix it.
